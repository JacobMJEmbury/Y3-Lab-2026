# Figures

Every script saves its figures here through `save_figure.py`. All restyled figures take their text size from `report_style` in `save_figure.py` (every piece of text the same size, 15 pt). Each run overwrites the previous PNGs, so this folder always matches the latest analysis. `fits_copy.py` (and `chi_squared.py`, which imports it) regenerates the `fits_*` figures. Each calibration script draws its `calib_*` figure only when you run it directly.

| File | Made by | What it shows |
|---|---|---|
| `calib_1_deadtime.png` | `deadtime.py` | GM-tube count rate against tube current, with the true rate N = kI and the non-paralysable dead-time fit (the tube is self-quenching). Gives τ. The paralysable and linearised 1/R vs 1/I fits are only printed. |
| `calib_2_zero_offset.png` | `angle_offset.py` | Direct-beam scan with no crystal (raw, not dead-time corrected) with its Gaussian fit. Dotted lines mark β = 0 and the fitted centre, with the gap between them (the goniometer zero offset β₀) shaded red and labelled. |
| `calib_3_alignment_error.png` | `alignment_error.py` | The Kα n=1 peak (from 15.7°) of the crystal turned 180° about the beam axis (red) and of the normal scan (blue, scaled to the same peak height), neither shifted, each with a Gaussian fit. Dotted lines mark the two fitted centres with the gap shaded red, and a box gives the overlay shift; half that shift is σ_align. The Gaussians are for display only. |
| `calib_4_temperature_error.png` | `temperature_error.py` | The Kα n=1 peak (from 15.7°, Tuesday's angle scale, before the angle fix) of the heated and unheated scans as measured, each with a Gaussian fit. Dotted lines mark the two fitted centres with the gap shaded red, and a box gives the overlay shift; the whole shift is σ_heat. The Gaussians are for display only. |
| `fits_1_full_spectrum.png` | `fits_copy.py` | The full Cu/NaCl Bragg spectrum with the dedicated 1st, 2nd and 3rd order Kα scans overlaid, each labelled with its tube current. The full scan is hidden where the 2nd and 3rd order scans cover it. All are dead-time and zero-offset corrected. |
| `fits_5_two_gaussians_free.png` | `fits_copy.py` | Two Gaussians fitted to each peak with every parameter free: Kα₁ (the lower-angle one), Kα₂ and their sum, with the Bragg's-law positions of Kα₁ and Kα₂ marked and arrows to the fitted lines. The fitted parameters are printed, not drawn. Its title sets the title size of figures 5, 6 and 8. In all three, the data points are grey, each panel spans the same number of peak widths as the 1st order, the sum of the lines is solid black, and the legends are 25% smaller and kept clear of the data. |
| `fits_6_two_gaussians_fixed_ratio.png` | `fits_copy.py` | Two Gaussians with I₂/I₁ fixed at 0.518 and a shared σ, with the Bragg's-law positions of Kα₁ and Kα₂ marked, with arrows to the fitted lines. This is the main fit used for the wavelength. The fitted parameters, their offsets from Bragg's law and the error budget of the Kα₁ centre are printed, not drawn. |
| `fits_8_pseudo_voigt.png` | `fits_copy.py` | Two pseudo-Voigts (I₂/I₁ fixed at 0.518, shared FWHM and η): Kα₁, Kα₂ and their sum, with the Bragg's-law positions marked as in fits_6. The χ²_ν of each line shape (against the Voigt fits), η and the offsets from Bragg's law are printed, not drawn. |
